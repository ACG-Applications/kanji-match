📖 How It Works

The Three Modes

  • 🎧 Listening: You hear a kana, tap its shape. Trains sound → shape.
  • 📖 Reading: You see a kana, tap a speaker tile to hear candidates, tap again to confirm. Trains shape → sound.
  • 🔀 Mixed: The app picks per kana based on which skill is weaker for you. Best once you've done some of both.

The Four Groups

Basic → Voiced → Yōon, unlocked in order. Voiced opens when most of Basic is solid; Yōon after Voiced.

Progress Labels

Under every card, you'll see a label like "New", "Just started", "Getting there", "Getting stronger", or "Solid". These are per-kana averages of your Reading and Listening mastery. No scores, no wrong counts — just where you are.

Why Some Kana Feel Harder

The grid is built from shape-confusable kana (シ/ツ/ソ/ン, き/さ/ち, etc.) on purpose. Tapping the wrong one is the app working as intended. Homophones (じ≡ぢ, ず≡づ) are always accepted either way.

Mixed Mode Tips

Direction is picked by mastery, capped at 3 in a row so it doesn't feel like a grind. If one skill lags, it'll show up more.

Audio

Uses your device's Japanese TTS voice. If it sounds off, install a Japanese voice in your OS settings. Auto-play can be turned off per-mode in the top bar.

Offline & Data

Everything works offline once loaded. Progress is saved on the device (localStorage), not synced.

⚠️

Reset Hiragana?
